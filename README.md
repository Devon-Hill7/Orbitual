# Orbitual

A habit tracker with AI-powered behavioral insights — currently in early planning.

The idea: most habit trackers just show you streaks. Orbitual will go further by using AI to analyze your patterns and surface actual insights about your behavior — why habits stick, when they break, and what to do about it.

**Planned features:**
- Log and track daily habits
- AI-generated insights based on your patterns over time
- Behavioral trend analysis

**Status:** Planned. Development hasn't started yet — other projects take priority. This one is on the roadmap.
